# Privacy Policy for IzonLearner

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

IzonLearner ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our language learning application.

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect information to provide and improve our services:
*   **Account Information:** Email, username, and password (hashed).
*   **Usage Data:** Learning progress, streaks, completed lessons, and interaction history.
*   **Device Data:** Information about the device, including operating system and network state.
*   **Permissions:** With your consent, we access:
    *   **Audio/Microphone:** For speech recognition and pronunciation practice.
    *   **Storage:** To cache learning content and save profile images.
    *   **Biometrics:** For secure authentication (if enabled).

## 2. How We Use Your Information
*   To manage your account and provide learning services.
*   To analyze learning progress and provide analytics.
*   To process language translations and learning queries using third-party AI services.
*   To improve app performance and stability.

## 3. Third-Party Services & AI
We use third-party services (e.g., AI models) to enhance language learning. When you interact with language features, your input may be processed by these services to generate translations or feedback. We ensure that our data usage complies with applicable data protection regulations.

## 4. Security
We implement robust security measures to protect your data, including hashed passwords, secure API communication (HTTPS), and restricted access to database credentials.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@lorek.com.
